- **NoDerivatives** — the files are **unmodified**. They were extracted from the

  change CC 3.0 allows as technically necessary to use the work in another
  format. They are not resized, cropped or feathered; `ShippedFileSizeTests`
  checks the sizes. Tinting them with `_C` happens when drawn and leaves the
  files alone.
